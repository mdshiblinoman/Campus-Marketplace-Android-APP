# Step 1: Identify the System Users

The first step in designing the Campus Marketplace system is to identify the main users and define their responsibilities. The system has three primary user types: Buyer, Seller, and Administrator.

## 1. Buyer

A buyer is a student who uses the marketplace to find and purchase products posted by other students.

Buyer responsibilities and functions:

- Register and log in using a university email.
- Browse available products.
- Search for products by keyword.
- Filter products by category and price.
- Sort product listings.
- View product details, including price, condition, seller information, and availability status.
- Add products to the wishlist.
- Contact sellers through buyer-seller chat.
- Receive chat and product notifications.
- Report suspicious, fake, duplicate, or inappropriate listings.

## 2. Seller

A seller is a student who can post products for sale. A seller can perform all buyer functions and also manage their own product listings.

Seller responsibilities and functions:

- Perform all buyer actions.
- Add new products.
- Upload product images.
- Edit product information such as price, description, category, condition, and location.
- Delete their own listings.
- Mark products as Available, Reserved, or Sold.
- Communicate with potential buyers through chat.
- Receive notifications about product status, messages, reports, and marketplace activity.
- Build trust through seller ratings and reviews after completed transactions.

## 3. Administrator

An administrator is responsible for maintaining the quality, safety, and reliability of the university marketplace.

Administrator responsibilities and functions:

- Access the admin dashboard.
- Manage registered users.
- Block or unblock suspicious users.
- Monitor product listings.
- Review newly submitted products before approval.
- Approve or reject product listings.
- Review reports submitted by users.
- Remove inappropriate, fake, or suspicious listings.
- Take action against reported listings or sellers.
- Send important admin notifications.
- Monitor marketplace activity, including users, listings, reports, approvals, sold products, and removed listings.

## Summary

The Campus Marketplace is designed around three roles:

- Buyer: discovers products and contacts sellers.
- Seller: manages listings and communicates with buyers.
- Administrator: moderates users, products, and reports.

This role separation keeps the system organized, improves marketplace safety, and supports the main workflows required for a university-specific marketplace.
